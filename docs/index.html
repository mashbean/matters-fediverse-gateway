01 · 成就

從概念驗證
走到正式服務

這個計畫把 Matters 的長文發佈能力接上開放的 ActivityPub 網路， 同時維持作者選擇權、公開內容邊界、原文網址與既有寫作流程。

01

一般作者正式開放

所有活躍作者都能在設定中開啟聯邦發佈。功能預設關閉，作者可以在每篇文章再次選擇是否送出。

02

文章生命週期完整同步

新文章送出 Create，修改文章送出 Update，封存或關閉聯邦發佈時送出 Delete，並維持穩定的文章物件識別。

03

跨服務身分可以被找到

每位啟用作者都有 @帳號@matters.town 聯邦身分，包含名稱、個人頁、頭像、收件匣與文章列表。

04

Threads 長文相容層

保留 Article 為主要物件，並只對 Threads 加送相容的 Note，讓遠端動態牆顯示文章並連回 Matters 原文。

05

雙向社交協定

Gateway 能處理追蹤、回覆、喜歡、轉發與取消等 ActivityPub 活動，並保存必要的跨站互動狀態。

06

正式維運能力

投遞佇列、重試、死信、備份、監控、告警、管理介面、回滾與客服文件已成為正式服務的一部分。

01 · Achievements

From proof of concept
to production

The project connects Matters' long-form publishing system to the open ActivityPub network while preserving author choice, public-content boundaries, canonical article URLs, and the existing writing workflow.

01

General-author access

Every active author can enable federation in settings. It is off by default, with an additional per-article delivery choice.

02

Complete article lifecycle

New articles emit Create, revisions emit Update, and archives or opt-outs emit Delete while retaining stable object identity.

03

Discoverable author identity

Each opted-in author receives an @handle@matters.town identity with name, profile, avatar, inbox, and outbox.

04

Threads long-form adapter

Article remains the primary object. A receiver-scoped Note companion makes the post visible in Threads and links back to the Matters original.

05

Bidirectional social protocol

The gateway handles follows, replies, likes, boosts, and undo activities while retaining the cross-server interaction state needed for recovery.

06

Production operations

Queues, retries, dead letters, backups, monitoring, alerts, admin controls, rollback, and support guidance are now part of the service.

02 · 功能

寫作不變
抵達的地方更多

作者不需要理解 ActivityPub，也不需要經營另一個後台。 聯邦發佈直接融入 Matters 的帳號設定、發文、修改與封存流程。

作

作者控制

聯邦發佈由作者主動開啟，每篇文章仍能獨立決定。

  • 帳號層級 opt-in
  • 文章層級繼承、開啟或關閉
  • 既有文章不會自動整批補送
文

文章同步

新公開文章會自動投遞，修改與封存也沿用同一個物件身分。

  • Create、Update、Delete
  • 保留 Matters 原文網址
  • Threads 專用 Note 相容層
聯

發現與互動

讀者可以從原本使用的聯邦服務搜尋作者、追蹤與閱讀文章。

  • WebFinger 與 NodeInfo
  • 作者頭像、個人頁與 outbox
  • 追蹤、回覆、喜歡、轉發與取消
守

內容邊界

在內容離開 Matters 前，來源端就會完成嚴格資格判斷。

  • 只投遞公開文章
  • 排除草稿、付費、圍爐與封存內容
  • 遠端副本與刪除限制清楚告知
02 · Features

Same writing flow
broader reach

Authors do not need to understand ActivityPub or operate another publishing console. Federation lives inside the existing Matters settings, publish, revise, and archive flows.

A

Author control

Federation is an explicit author choice, and each article remains independently configurable.

  • Account-level opt-in
  • Per-article inherit, enable, or disable
  • No automatic bulk backfill of older work
P

Article synchronization

New public articles are delivered automatically. Revisions and archives retain the same object identity.

  • Create, Update, and Delete
  • Canonical Matters article URL
  • Threads-scoped Note compatibility
F

Discovery and interaction

Readers can find and follow Matters authors from the federated services they already use.

  • WebFinger and NodeInfo
  • Avatar, profile, and outbox
  • Follow, reply, like, boost, and undo
S

Content boundaries

Strict source-side eligibility runs before any content can leave Matters.

  • Public articles only
  • Draft, paid, circle, and archived content excluded
  • Clear remote-copy and deletion disclosure

05 · Network Fediverse

Mastodon · Threads · Misskey · ActivityPub

Cloudflare Worker 只接管 WebFinger、NodeInfo、actor、object 與 inbox 等聯邦路徑，Matters 主站維持原有服務。 Routes only WebFinger, NodeInfo, actor, object, and inbox traffic while the main Matters site remains unchanged.
SQLite + Backup 保存 actor、追蹤、投遞、互動與稽核狀態，並定期離線備份。 Persists actor, follow, delivery, interaction, and audit state with scheduled offline backup.
Operations CloudWatch、計時器、管理介面、重送、結案與回滾。 CloudWatch, timers, admin tools, replay, resolution, and rollback.

發文路徑由左至右。遠端搜尋、讀取、追蹤與互動則透過 Cloudflare Worker 回到 gateway-core。 出站與入站共享同一套 actor 身分、簽章與稽核狀態。

Publishing flows from left to right. Remote discovery, reads, follows, and interactions return through the Cloudflare Worker to gateway-core. Inbound and outbound traffic share the same actor identity, signatures, and audit state.

04 · 撞到的牆

協定相同
行為仍然不同

ActivityPub 提供共同語言，但真正的互通發生在服務實作、網路安全、 快取、內容模型與維運習慣的交界。以下是正式上線前最重要的六道牆。

Discovery

看得到路由，不代表找得到帳號

WebFinger、NodeInfo、actor 與個人頁必須彼此一致。Cloudflare 挑戰規則曾攔下遠端伺服器，Threads 能找到帳號時，Mastodon 仍可能完全查無結果。

Compatibility

Article 並非每個動態牆都會顯示

Threads 能解析身分，卻不一定把長文 Article 放進動態牆。最後保留標準 Article，再為 Threads 加上限縮接收端的 Note companion。

Identity

識別、頭像與金鑰都有快取

遠端會快取 actor、頭像、公開金鑰與 object。修正資料後不一定立即可見，金鑰版本、canonical URL 與 profile URL 必須從一開始就穩定。

Lifecycle

刪除必須指向同一個物件

Create、Update 與 Delete 只要有一次 object ID 漂移，遠端就可能留下舊文章。最後一個正式修正就是讓封存事件永遠使用原始 Article canonical ID。

Reliability

部署成功不等於遠端收到

Lambda、SQS 與 Gateway 健康，只能證明本地管線運作。真正驗收還要確認投遞回應、遠端畫面、修改結果與刪除結果，並將重試與死信分開處理。

Network safety

安全防護也可能擋住正常聯邦流量

SSRF 防護、未知 ASN、IPv4-mapped IPv6 與接收端網路行為曾造成真實投遞失敗。最後採用窄路由例外、嚴格目的地驗證與可觀察重試。

04 · Walls we hit

One protocol
many behaviors

ActivityPub provides a shared language, but real interoperability lives at the boundaries between implementations, network security, caches, content models, and operations. Six walls defined the path to production.

Discovery

A working route does not guarantee discovery

WebFinger, NodeInfo, actor documents, and profile pages must agree. Cloudflare challenges once blocked remote servers, and Mastodon could still fail discovery after Threads found the same account.

Compatibility

Not every feed renders Article

Threads could resolve an actor without placing its long-form Article in the feed. The final design preserves the standard Article and adds a receiver-scoped Note companion for Threads.

Identity

Identity, avatars, and keys are cached

Remote servers cache actors, avatars, public keys, and objects. Corrections are rarely instant, so key versions, canonical URLs, and profile URLs must remain stable from the start.

Lifecycle

Delete must reference the same object

Any object-ID drift across Create, Update, and Delete can leave an old article visible. The final production fix made archive events preserve the original canonical Article ID.

Reliability

A healthy deployment is not remote delivery

Healthy Lambda, SQS, and gateway services prove only the local pipeline. Acceptance also required delivery responses, remote UI readback, update visibility, deletion visibility, retries, and dead-letter review.

Network safety

Security controls can block valid federation

SSRF protection, unknown ASNs, IPv4-mapped IPv6, and receiver networking behavior caused real delivery failures. Narrow route exceptions, strict destination checks, and observable retries resolved the conflict.

  • Threads 等服務逐步開放的遠端回覆與分享介面
06 · Operations

Build complete
eyes open

Production means the core scope has passed acceptance. The Fediverse is still made of independent services, so operations must combine reliable delivery, safe rollback, and clear disclosure of receiver-side limits.

What Matters controls

  • Public eligibility and author choice before content leaves Matters
  • Ordered queues, partial-batch failures, five-minute retries, and dead-letter resolution
  • Alerts for gateway heartbeat, backup, Lambda, SQS, EC2, and delivery age
  • Admin inspection, replay, resolution, audit, and safe rollback
  • Support through hi@matters.town with incident severity guidance

What remote services control

  • When a new remote account or article is indexed
  • How long-form objects, summaries, images, and layout are rendered
  • When an Update appears through existing caches
  • When Delete is processed and whether caches, backups, or boosts remain
  • Phased availability of remote reply and share interfaces on services such as Threads
